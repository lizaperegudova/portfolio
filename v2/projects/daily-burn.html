01 Overview

From a workout library to a connected experience

Daily Burn offered a large library of workouts and training programs. The team explored a broader fitness ecosystem that could support motivation and connection as well as exercise. The opportunity was especially relevant to an active Facebook community that had no way to encourage one another or share progress inside the app.

02 Scope

Research-led product design across platforms

I worked with a second designer, dividing the work by feature. We shaped the new direction using a product hypothesis, interviews, surveys, and product analytics.

Connected fitness

Explored ways for members to support one another, share achievements, and follow friends' progress through an in-app scoreboard.

Progress & analytics

Expanded the product vision with workout history, personal achievements, and progress indicators such as weight and BMI.

More ways to engage

The concept also brought together nutrition content and trainer stories, plus live workout data such as heart rate and blood oxygen alongside in-the-moment chat.

Design system

Maintained the existing design system and developed it to support the new product direction.

Achievements & sharing

Created the achievement visuals and designed the flow for sharing them.

Roku first-time experience

Conducted research into the Roku app's first-time user experience and facilitated a CJM workshop.

Platform coverage

Designed across iOS, Android, web, and Roku, working with the team to carry the product vision across platforms.

03 Highlight

Bringing community into the product

Interviews, surveys, and analytics informed a product hypothesis: members who already formed an active community on Facebook needed a place in Daily Burn to encourage each other and share their progress. The new vision connected training with social motivation, personal progress, and nutrition content.

The design work covered iOS, Android, web, and Roku. For Roku, I conducted research into the first-time user experience and facilitated a CJM workshop to map the user journey.

04 Illustration

Achievement visuals made to be shared

I created the achievement visuals and designed the sharing flow, giving members a way to celebrate their progress and share it with others.

05 Outcome

A complete design vision, partially implemented

The new product direction was fully designed, and implementation began across platforms. The project changed ownership before the work could be completed and tested, so there were no post-launch results to report.
